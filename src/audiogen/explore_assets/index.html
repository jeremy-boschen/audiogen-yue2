YuE2 generation microscope · Burn It Down

Watch a song being made

Three interactive 3D views of one take and its siblings: the acoustic solve resolving from noise, the four layers the model writes on one time axis, and every finished take placed by how its sound moves. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, and every view plays the audio it shows.

01 · the headline

Coming into focus

The song drawn as a landscape of sound at each of the 32 steps the model takes from pure noise to the finished take. Move through the steps while it plays, and hear where the model thinks it is heading.
02 · the hierarchy

The stack

The four layers the model writes, as floors of one building: the score it plans, a sketch of the sound, the detailed sound, and the audio. Light one moment through all of them, hear the phrase, read its lyric, and replay the writing as it happened.
03 · all the takes

Take map

Every finished render as a glowing point, placed by envelope similarity: seed studies, fixed-ABC takes, noise seeds and N-step solves. Click to listen; switch takes at the same moment.

What the numbers are

The numbers on these pages measure the sound as a signal: how closely each step matches the finished song, and where it changes. They show what changes and when; they do not say whether it sounds good. Your ears decide that.
